About

My current research lies at the intersection of low-resource language technologies, machine translation, large language models, and speech processing. I aim to develop practical and culturally grounded AI systems that address the unique challenges of underrepresented languages, particularly Vietnamese.

Research interests

Speech-to-speech translation · Vietnamese NLP · Language safety · Large language models

I’d be happy to discuss my research, potential collaborations, or related topics via email.
